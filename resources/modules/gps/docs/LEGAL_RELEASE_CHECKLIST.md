# Legal / Protection Release Checklist

Owner contact: `enorrial@gmail.com`
Owner Discord: `klon_92`

Use this before publishing EnorialsGPS publicly.

## 1) Legal files

- Keep `LICENSE` in release source package.
- Keep `EULA.md` in release source package.
- Keep `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in release source package.

## 2) Distribution hygiene

- Publish only from your official channel.
- Publish checksums/hash of the jar.
- Avoid bundling third-party premium plugins/assets.
- Include explicit "not affiliated with third-party plugin authors" note.

## 3) Practical limitation

No local-only plugin can be 100% piracy-proof.
Legal documents improve your legal position, but cannot guarantee prevention of
all unauthorized use.
